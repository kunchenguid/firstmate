The forensics' only stable rule type: what was abolished stayed abolished,
while eleven correct prose rules were broken in two days. This file is
append-only and monotonically growing - that is its job.

It is NEVER injected into any session, brief, or hook. Its only machine
reader is bin/fm-regel-eval.sh (re-introduction lock: a new rule closely
matching an entry here needs the captain's word). Humans read it when they
wonder "why don't we have a rule for X" - the answer is usually here.

Format: `- <date> <id-or-name> - <what it was> (Anker: ...; Grund: <why it fell>)`
