# auto-research evolution

Distilled lessons from campaigns. Dated, evidence-backed, pruned when superseded.

- 2026-09-23 (EXP3 Garfield campaign): The lead-scout paradigm works — a persistent lead crewmate drove the campaign while a Grok scout ran a bounded literature survey in parallel; routing the scout's report back to the lead produced immediate, well-gated follow-up work.
- 2026-09-23: A `when`-watch on the lead's pane (idle → wake firstmate → push) keeps a campaign moving without captain attention. Mechanism and environment pitfalls are owned by `process-event-sources` and `data/learnings.md` (2026-09-23 entry) — cross-reference, do not duplicate.
- 2026-09-23: Gate cures behind diagnostics. The lead correctly staged mitigation variants (din/uniform/residual-gate) as opt-in configs but refused to queue them until the diagnostic ablation confirmed the mechanism — burning GPU on unconfirmed cures is waste.
- 2026-09-23: Pre-register decision tables and pre-draft interpretation sections while runs are in flight; when results land, analysis becomes mechanical instead of starting cold.
- 2026-09-23: Verify novelty claims against literature before writing them — the "conditional sink" framing survived only after checking Guo/Barbero/Ran-Milo/Gu prior art; the honest position was a new diagnostic use of a known phenomenon.
- 2026-09-23 (council verdict): The skill's fatal gap was durable campaign state, not philosophy. Resolution: science state in the vault's existing index chain, fleet handles in firstmate records (`state/<id>.meta` + one `data/` campaign record), and the seam is a stable pointer line on the vault's startup file written by the lead — never handles stamped into the vault. A `shared/` convention beside an existing index is dead weight.
