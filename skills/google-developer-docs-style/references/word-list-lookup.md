# Word list lookup

Use the official [Google developer documentation word list](https://developers.google.com/style/word-list) as the spelling and usage owner for technical documentation terms.

## Procedure

1. Search the word list for the exact term or close variants.
2. If the term appears, follow the listed spelling, capitalization, hyphenation, and usage notes.
3. If the term is absent, open [Merriam-Webster](https://www.merriam-webster.com/) for spelling.
   When Merriam-Webster lists multiple spellings, use the first form unless project style says otherwise.
4. For nontechnical grammar or punctuation not covered by the Google guide, consult *The Chicago Manual of Style*.
5. For technical phrasing the Google guide does not cover, consult the [Microsoft Writing Style Guide](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/style-guide/welcome/) when it fits your product domain.

## Do not vendor

- Do not copy word-list entries into skills, AGENTS.md, or project docs.
- Do not maintain a parallel glossary unless the project explicitly owns product-specific terms outside the Google list.
- Record only project-specific exceptions in the project's style owner, with a pointer back to the official list for everything else.

## Product-specific terms

When the word list marks a term as product-specific or points to platform logos (Android, Google Cloud), apply that platform section only when you are documenting that platform.
Otherwise prefer the general entry.
