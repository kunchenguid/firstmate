### Autopilot-full

**You own the verdicts, never the merges. One owner runs each PR from build to merge-ready, and nothing is declared ready without your clean verifier verdict.** For "autopilot this queue", "full autopilot", and one-owner-per-PR programs. The job is a queue of independent PRs driven to merge-ready with full autonomy inside the brief. The root keeps verification, countersigns, and audits; landing belongs to the merge authority on its explicit go-ahead.

1. **Mark the supervisor's items and honor state-then-wait.** Items the supervisor names stay gated: no owner touches one past merge-ready, and landing waits for the merge authority. When asked to state the protocol or the plan, deliver the statement and stop. Execution starts only on explicit go.
2. **Spawn one owner per PR with the full build-and-verify lifecycle.** One worker per PR owns build, branch registration, self-proof on the real artifact (the **prove-it-works** principle skill), skeptical reviewer-feedback triage, a slop-strip (the **unslop** skill), **no-comments** (the **no-comments** skill), a rebase onto current trunk, and the babysit loop to green (verify only; landing belongs to the merge authority) per `babysit.md`. The rebase always precedes babysit and never waits for drift or conflicts. Every owner keeps a decisions.tsv trail per the **show-me-your-work** skill, never committed, returned with its reports. No owner merges, arms merging, or closes; step 4 gates the verdict.
3. **Run owners in true parallel and never stack.** Many owners at once when PRs are self-contained: one writer per branch, disjoint files, cross-PR drift absorbed by rebase. Only genuinely overlapping work serializes. Self-contained PRs branch straight off main, and sequenced work is merge-then-branch. the stack-visibility rule is about review order, not tooling. One exception: an owner that must split a genuinely dependent change may hold a short private stack.
4. **Verifier-fan-out every merge-ready head before declaring it ready.** At the owner's merge-ready head SHA, fan out parallel independent verifier passes and aggregate to one verdict. Keep the lanes independent: separate outputs, one aggregated verdict. The lanes: re-run the gates at that SHA; prove the load-bearing behavior live on the real surface the change touches; audit the receipts and the diff, distrusting the PR body. The live lane is the floor, and a verdict without it is not clean. No ready declaration without the root's clean verdict. Findings go back to the owner for fix-forward, and the new head gets a fresh fan-out and a fresh verdict.
5. **On a clean verdict the owner reports merge-ready and takes the next item.** The merge-ready report is made at a trunk-current head, and the verifier verdict pins that SHA. If trunk moves again before landing, the patch-id rule in `shipping.md` governs re-verification; a new head voids the verdict unless the patch-id is unchanged. The owner then picks up its next self-contained item from the queue. A clean verdict is a readiness declaration, never a merge authorization: landing waits for the merge authority. Supervisor-named items stop at merge-ready and wait.
6. **Run the root layer.** A genuinely new raise of a pinned gate or budget value (a limit CI only lets tighten) needs your fresh countersign, granted only after verifier proof. Absorbing values that already landed on main is drift, not a raise. Run an audit tick over all owners roughly every 30 minutes on a supervisor recheck chain. Each tick probes worker liveness via the supervisor, audits both progress and protocol adherence, and collects the decision trails. After a batch lands, run a retro pass and a post-merge review-comment sweep.
7. **Stand down instantly on the supervisor's stop.** The hold or stand-down reaches every owner as a zero-writes order immediately. Owners hold their briefs until released.

**Reply:** the queue with each PR's owner, state, and head SHA; each verdict and the fan-out that produced it; what was declared ready and what each owner took next; countersigns granted and why; open supervisor gates; where the collected decision trails live.
